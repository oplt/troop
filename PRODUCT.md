# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Troop serves AI engineering teams, workflow builders, operators, and administrators who coordinate long-running agent work in a shared operational environment. Builders configure projects, agents, prompts, knowledge, and evaluations. Operators monitor tasks, resolve approvals, inspect evidence, and recover failed work. Administrators govern access, integrations, policy, cost, and auditability.

Users are usually working inside a dense desktop application and need to understand system state quickly. Their primary job is to move AI-assisted work from intent to a traceable, approved outcome without losing context or control.

## Product Purpose

Troop is an operational workspace for persistent, memory-aware AI agents. It unifies project and task management, orchestration, knowledge retrieval, evaluation, approvals, execution history, and organizational governance.

Success means a user can start or inspect work, understand what evidence and rules informed it, intervene when needed, and replay or evaluate the result without navigating a collection of disconnected tools. The product favors controllable workflows over unconstrained autonomy.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, and capable. Troop should feel engineered for serious operational work while remaining approachable to people who are not specialists in vector search or agent internals. Copy is concise, direct, and evidence-oriented.

## Anti-references

- Chat-first agent demos that hide workflow state, provenance, and failure recovery.
- Generic admin dashboards made from repeated cards with no clear primary action.
- Interfaces that expose infrastructure vocabulary, such as embeddings or raw vector scores, to ordinary operators.
- Dense enterprise software that presents every capability in global navigation.
- Decorative AI styling, gratuitous gradients, glass effects, or animation that competes with the task.

## Design Principles

1. Put work in context. Project, task, agent, run, and company surfaces own their related capabilities.
2. Reveal complexity progressively. Ordinary users see outcomes and evidence; advanced controls stay available on demand.
3. Make system state legible. Every asynchronous action shows its lifecycle, recovery path, and most useful next action.
4. Preserve trust. Provenance, permissions, approval boundaries, and audit history are visible wherever AI output can affect work.
5. Optimize for operational flow. Familiar patterns, targeted refreshes, and inspector views help users stay oriented.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. All product workflows must support keyboard navigation, visible focus, descriptive control labels, non-color status cues, screen-reader semantics, sufficient contrast, and reduced-motion preferences. Dense desktop workflows must retain a usable small-screen fallback without hiding critical state or recovery actions.
