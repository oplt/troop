You are a **principal AI platform engineer, staff full-stack engineer, product architect, and database migration specialist** working directly on:

`https://github.com/oplt/troop`

Your task is to evolve the existing Troop application from a primarily GitHub/software-development-oriented AI orchestration system into a **generic task-driven AI workforce platform** where users define their own projects, tasks, organizational departments, skills, agents, tools, workflows, and knowledge.

Do **not** build separate hard-coded Sales, Marketing, R&D, HR, Legal, Engineering modules.

The architecture must instead revolve around:

```text
Organization
    │
    ├── User-defined Departments / Organizational Units
    │
    ├── Projects
    │      ├── Tasks
    │      ├── Knowledge
    │      ├── Agents
    │      ├── Skills
    │      ├── Tools / Integrations
    │      ├── Workflows
    │      └── Artifacts
    │
    ├── Agents
    ├── Skills
    ├── Tools
    ├── Workflows
    ├── Knowledge
    ├── Policies
    └── Evaluations
```

The central product principle is:

> Users describe work. Troop understands what capabilities that work requires, discovers or generates reusable skills, identifies or assembles appropriate agents, assigns the work, executes it through governed tools, evaluates the result, and learns reusable capabilities from repeated work.

# 58. DESIGN PRINCIPLES THAT MUST NOT BE VIOLATED

1. **Projects/tasks are the center of the product.**
2. Departments are user-defined context, not hard-coded modules.
3. Tasks determine required capabilities.
4. Capabilities determine skills.
5. Skills help determine agents.
6. Skills are reusable and versioned.
7. Generated skills begin as drafts.
8. Skill generation must detect duplicates.
9. Skill scope prevents registry pollution.
10. Successful skills can be promoted.
11. Agents are compositions of reusable skills.
12. Tools require explicit permissions.
13. Generated prompts cannot grant permissions.
14. Workflows combine deterministic and agentic steps.
15. GitHub is an integration/workload, not the platform architecture.
16. Important domain data should not live only in metadata JSON.
17. Existing functionality must be migrated, not casually discarded.
18. Every recommendation must be explainable.
19. Every AI-generated structured object must be validated.
20. Every significant execution must be auditable.
